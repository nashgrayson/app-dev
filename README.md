# My favorite Manga/Series: Steel Ball Run

## About the Series
**Steel Ball Run** is a bizarre story about a race of horses. 
From San Diego, California to New York City, but the race has an uxepected
happenings where the President of the United States search for a missing pieces of corpse and
starts sending some assassins to get the piece that Johnny Joestar have,
if he collects all of the pieces, the president will use the Corpse to make America the luckiest 
and most powerful nation, even if other people suffer because of it.
